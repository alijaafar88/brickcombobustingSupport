Brick Busting

Tap groups of matching bricks to bust them. The bigger the combo, the more it's worth. Back for the first time since 2012, rebuilt from the ground up.

  • Brick Busting: bust what you can, swipe to slide the bricks together
  • Combo Busting: build huge combos before your moves run out
  • Survival: clear the whole board, again and again
  • Four powers: Duh Bomb, Duh Laser, Color Changer and Color Destroyer
  • Earn coins as you play and spend them in the shop
  • Plays fully offline, in 60 languages
